Midnight Club: Los Angeles on your Switch

This page makes the SD card files for MCLA-NX from your own disc image of the game. The image is read here, in this browser: nothing is uploaded.

  1. 1

    Choose your disc image

    The Xbox 360 disc of Midnight Club: Los Angeles Complete Edition, as an .iso file.

  2. 2

    Choose where the files go

    Write them straight to your SD card, or save one ZIP file to unpack onto the SD card later.

  3. 3

    Copy

    Keep this page open until it finishes. The game files are about 6 GB.

    0 GB of 0 GB
  4. 4

    Play

    1. Unpack the ZIP at the root of the SD card. It holds a switch folder.
    2. Put the SD card back in the Switch. In Sphaira, make a forwarder for mcla-nx.nro with the 39-bit address space and 4 CPUs (the game needs that much memory).
    3. Start the game from its forwarder. Settings are in the pause menu and saved in mcla.toml. The overclocking notes say which clocks it needs.

About this page

You need your own copy of the game. The files are made from it on this device; the program itself (MCLA-NX) comes with this page. Only the USA/Europe disc (title ID 545407F8) works.

Writing straight to the SD card works in Chrome and Edge on a computer. Everywhere else, the ZIP is saved by the browser's own download.
